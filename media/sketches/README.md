# Design Notebook Pages

Scanned pages from the hand-written design notebook kept during this build, referenced from `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/ELECTRONICS.md` as supporting evidence for specific engineering decisions.

- `sketch-weight-budget.png` — thrust/power/weight budget calculations used to set a mass ceiling before modeling the frame
- `sketch-frame-geometry.png` — arm-length and prop-clearance geometry
- `sketch-joint-design.png` — brass heat-set insert sizing and the screw/nut/peg joint concept
- `sketch-battery-mount-tradeoff.png` — top-vs-bottom battery mounting trade study
- `sketch-channel-map.png` — transmitter channel-to-stick mapping notes

These are working notes, not polished figures — kept as-is because they show real engineering reasoning rather than a cleaned-up retelling of it.
